Methodology

How to read this dashboard

Dashboard and story visuals are embedded from Tableau Public. The supporting stats and table on this page are illustrative sample data, structured to match how EV market reporting is normally sliced.

A

Scope

Passenger light-duty vehicles only. Commercial fleets, buses and two/three-wheelers are excluded from the sales series.

B

Definitions

“EV” means battery-electric plus plug-in hybrid. Conventional hybrids are counted as combustion vehicles.

C

Refresh cadence

Annual totals with quarterly partial updates. Charging figures count publicly accessible connectors only.
